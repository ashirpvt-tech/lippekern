# Lippekern visual identity

Lippekern is a parent technology company, with Jobwelt as its first featured product. Its identity must be able to support future AI, software, infrastructure and information products.

## Direction

An editorial, light European technology identity: generous space, a disciplined grid, medium-weight typography and forest-green accents. The layered core artwork is original SVG geometry. The nested hexagonal brand mark represents systems connected around a common core and remains legible as a monochrome icon.

## Tokens

- Paper: `#fafbf7`
- Ink: `#202e29`
- Forest: `#173f32`
- Sage: `#eef2e9`
- Typography: self-hosted Manrope Variable, with system sans-serif fallback.
- Content: 1240 px maximum at ordinary desktop sizes, 18–56 px adaptive gutters.
- Corners: restrained 4–10 px radii; no pervasive pill cards.
- Motion: 200 ms hover transitions, gentle one-time section reveals, slow SVG float. Reduced-motion preferences disable these effects.

## Content and interaction

German is the default. Both languages have server-rendered pages, metadata, legal drafts and all form states. The sticky header uses a disclosure menu on small screens. Focus, skip navigation, native labels and error summaries must remain intact.

Jobwelt uses a genuine dated website capture, plus its real SVG icon retrieved from the public header. Lippekern does not adopt Jobwelt's purple product identity. No invented claims, metrics or social proof may be added.

The proposed UG remains in formation until company details are confirmed. Contact delivery stays visibly disabled until the delivery and abuse-protection services are configured.
